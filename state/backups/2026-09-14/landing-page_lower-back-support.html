    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I know what size to order?

    Measure around your waist at the belly button and match it to our size chart. The belt runs snug and is highly adjustable, so if you're between sizes, size up — you can always tighten it down.

    Can I wear it under my clothes?

    Yes. It's slim and breathable, and it sits comfortably under a loose shirt, sweater, or jacket without bulging out.

    How long can I wear it each day?

    Everyone's different — many people start with an hour or two and build up from there, and it's smart to take it off for breaks so your muscles stay active. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor about what's right for you.

    Who is this belt for?

    It's made for everyday support — long shifts on your feet, desk-bound days, lifting, and general back fatigue. It's a support belt, not a medical device, and it isn't a substitute for professional care.

    What's your return policy?

    You've got 60 days. If it doesn't fit or isn't for you, send it back for a refund — just keep it in original condition.
